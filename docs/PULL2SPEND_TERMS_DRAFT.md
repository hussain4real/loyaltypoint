## 1. Who provides Pull2Spend

Pull2Spend is operated by **[LEGAL OPERATOR NAME]**, registered under **[REGISTRATION NUMBER AND JURISDICTION]**, at **[REGISTERED ADDRESS]**. In these Terms, "we", "us" and "our" mean that operator. Contact us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL OR IN-APP CHANNEL]**.

These Terms govern your use of the Pull2Spend app and the features made available to you. Your Loyalty Partner's terms continue to govern your loyalty account and points. Before a wallet or card is activated, we will identify its legal provider and give you the applicable wallet/card agreement, fees, complaints route and funds-protection information. We do not represent that Pull2Spend itself holds a payment-services or banking licence.

## 2. Eligibility and registration

You must be at least 18, legally able to enter this agreement, and within the countries and customer categories supported by the service. Use your own identity and accounts. Provide accurate information and tell us when your contact or identity details change.

Registration requires a verified contact and acceptance of the current Terms. The Privacy Notice explains the proposed use of personal data. Reading or acknowledging that notice is not permission for every type of data processing. Where separate consent is required, we will request it before the relevant activity. Marketing is optional and is not a condition of registration.

Contact verification alone does not approve identity checks, create spendable funds or qualify you for a card.

## 3. Identity checks and account security

Before financial access, we require the applicable document-authenticity, live-selfie, identity and sanctions/PEP/adverse-media checks. We may request additional information or fresh verification. An unavailable check, an unresolved match or a pending review does not count as approval. You may ask for review through **[REVIEW/SUPPORT CHANNEL]**, subject to lawful restrictions on what we can disclose.

Keep passwords, verification codes and devices secure. Do not share credentials or approve an action you do not understand. Device biometric login is optional where supported; password fallback remains available. Device biometric login does not replace identity verification. Report suspected account misuse or a lost device promptly through **[URGENT SECURITY CHANNEL]**.

We may require fresh authentication for sensitive actions and lock inactive or backgrounded sessions. A successful sign-in does not remove account or financial restrictions.

## 4. Connecting a Loyalty Partner

Connect only a loyalty account you own and are entitled to use. We will describe the requested access and obtain your permission for the connection. An email match alone does not establish ownership or authority to redeem points.

The Loyalty Partner remains authoritative for points balances, eligibility and the monetary value it offers. Displayed balances may include a timestamp or a stale-data warning. A displayed balance is not a guaranteed redemption offer.

You may disconnect an account through the available controls or support. Disconnecting stops future access under that connection; it does not cancel an instruction already committed or remove records we must retain.

## 5. Redemption offers and your approval

The Loyalty Partner supplies the points quantity and equivalent monetary value. Pull2Spend does not set or calculate the partner's point-to-money conversion rules.

Before you approve an executable redemption, we will show the points quantity, partner-supplied amount and currency, applicable consumer fees, wallet credit and any offer expiry or validity conditions supplied by the partner. We retain the relevant quote/transaction reference and confirmation evidence. An offer must receive authoritative confirmation before commitment.

If the offered value changes, including an increase, we will present the confirmed replacement offer and updated allocations for your fresh approval before commitment. We will not silently treat a different currency as your wallet currency or introduce an undisclosed currency conversion.

For an offer involving several partners, you approve the combined terms presented for that offer. We will not silently replace a failed leg with a different allocation or leave you with an unapproved partial redemption. Where completion or recovery is uncertain, the transaction stays pending or enters an exception process until its outcome is confirmed.

Approval submits an instruction under the displayed terms. Once a partner commits a redemption, cancellation may no longer be possible. A timeout or error message does not prove that no points were debited; check the recorded status or contact support before repeating an instruction.

## 6. Fees and wallet credit

The fee arrangement may differ by partner and product. We disclose the fee you bear, how it is applied and your wallet credit before approval. Where the partner bears a separately billed fee, that fee is not also deducted from your credit unless the presented terms explicitly provide for it.

Illustration only: **10,000 points with a supplied monetary value of QAR 350.00.** Under a deducted platform fee of 12%, the fee is QAR 42.00 and wallet credit is QAR 308.00. This is not a universal rate or a standing offer. Separate partner incentive payments do not replace the point-funding obligations needed to back wallet value.

We will give you a transaction reference and the confirmed amounts when an operation completes. We will not create spendable credit from an unconfirmed offer or from development test data.

## 7. Wallet and virtual-card use

Wallet/card access depends on completed verification, available funded value, the provider's eligibility rules and the agreements disclosed before activation. The approved operating currency will be stated before use. No deposit-insurance, interest, cash-withdrawal or external cash-out entitlement is created by these Terms.

Card authorizations can temporarily reduce your available balance before final capture. Refunds, reversals, disputes and delayed captures are processed under the applicable issuer and scheme rules. We will show their status; a pending refund is not automatically available to spend. Apple Pay and Google Pay availability depends on the issuer, device, market and applicable certification.

Separate issuer terms must identify the issuer's responsibilities, applicable fees, funds protection, balance-return process and statutory rights. These Terms do not replace those disclosures.

## 8. Restricted use and service restrictions

Do not use Pull2Spend for fraud, impersonation, unlawful transactions, sanctions evasion, unauthorized account access or attempts to bypass verification, transaction limits or security controls.

We may restrict new actions where required for security, verification, law, an issuer instruction or investigation. Where permitted, we will explain the restriction and available remediation. We may be unable to disclose information that would compromise a lawful investigation or another person's rights.

A restriction does not erase a valid balance or an existing obligation. We continue the applicable recovery, reconciliation, dispute and settlement processes for committed activity.

## 9. Errors, unauthorized activity and complaints

Report an incorrect redemption, unexpected debit, account compromise or disputed card transaction promptly through **[SUPPORT/URGENT CONTACT]**. Provide the transaction reference and a description; do not send passwords, verification codes or full card credentials.

We will record your case and explain the next steps. Card disputes may require referral to the issuer. Applicable issuer/scheme deadlines and statutory rights will be disclosed before activation; contact us promptly rather than waiting for a general support response.

If a complaint remains unresolved, use **[FORMAL COMPLAINTS ADDRESS]**, then **[APPLICABLE REGULATOR OR EXTERNAL DISPUTE ROUTE]**. The final terms must state the applicable acknowledgment, response and escalation timeframes. Nothing here waives mandatory consumer remedies or makes our records conclusive against you.

## 10. Closing your account

You may request closure through **[ACCOUNT CLOSURE CHANNEL]**. We will explain any pending transaction, dispute, lawful restriction or obligation that affects the process, and the applicable provider's route for returning eligible remaining value.

Closure does not itself forfeit a valid balance, reverse completed redemptions or erase records retained for lawful purposes. No closure charge or balance-return deduction applies unless lawfully agreed and disclosed in the applicable terms.

## 11. Changes, availability and responsibility

We may change supported features or these Terms. We will identify a new version, explain material changes and provide the notice or fresh agreement required by applicable law and the relevant provider agreement. We will explain available closure options if you do not accept a material change. An urgent security restriction may take effect sooner where permitted.

Service interruptions and external provider failures can delay an operation. We will retain a traceable status and handle unresolved commitments rather than reporting uncertain outcomes as complete. We remain responsible for duties imposed on us by applicable law and our agreements. Nothing excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, or removes mandatory rights concerning unauthorized or incorrectly executed transactions.

## 12. Governing terms and law

These Terms are subject to **[CONFIRMED GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE FORUM]**. Mandatory consumer rights continue to apply. The wallet/card agreement governs the provider-specific service it describes; any priority or conflict rule must be clearly disclosed and cannot override mandatory law.

The service will identify its supported languages. Before release, we will publish reviewed English and Arabic versions and state **[APPROVED LANGUAGE-PRECEDENCE RULE]**.

## 13. Registration wording and version record

Proposed registration wording: **"I agree to the Pull2Spend Terms of Use and acknowledge that I have read the Privacy Notice."** You can read both documents before agreeing. Marketing and any separately required identity/biometric consent have their own choices.

We keep a record of the Terms and Privacy Notice versions presented to you and the time of your agreement. You may request the text relevant to that record through support.
